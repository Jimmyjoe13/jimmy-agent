Jimy 0.2.4: a soul that learns from you

Published October 28, 2026 · By Jimmyjoe13 · 4 min read · Lire en français

Jimy is an open source desktop AI agent for Windows that you drive by voice or text. Until now, its personality was written once and for all in its code: when you corrected it, you had to say it again next time. With 0.2.4, Jimy has a soul, and it evolves it on its own.

In short

  • A soul that learns: when you correct how Jimy answers or works, it draws a lesson and keeps it for every conversation that follows.
  • You stay in control: Settings › Soul to review what it learned, edit it or undo it.
  • "Summarize my day" works: Jimy finds your actual conversations of the day instead of improvising.
  • Since 0.2.2: a conversation list next to the Chat, four voice engines including a free offline one, a complete English translation, tasks that survive a restart.

A soul Jimy evolves on its own

The idea comes from the SOUL.md file popularized by the open source agent Hermes: a short text that says who the agent is and how it likes to work, read at every exchange. Jimy's contains its character and a list of learned lessons, one sentence each.

  • You correct it, it remembers. "Give me the number before the explanation", "stop asking me to confirm that": Jimy writes the lesson into its soul, tells you in one sentence, then sticks to it everywhere, in text and by voice.
  • It also learns from its failures. Its periodic review goes over its tool errors; when a work habit emerges, it joins the lessons.
  • A short soul, on purpose. It is read with every message, so it is capped at 2,500 characters. Rather than piling up, Jimy has to condense or replace a lesson. The cost of each exchange stays almost the same.

What the soul cannot change: Jimy's safety rules (your approval before writing a sensitive file, sending a message, shutting down the PC…) stay in its core, written in the code. In case of conflict, the core always wins. The soul can't hold a key or a password either.

Everything stays visible and reversible

In Settings › Soul, you read Jimy's soul as it sees it, with its recent changes: who made them, when, and why. You can rewrite it yourself, undo its last change or go back to the original soul. A copy of the previous version is kept before every change.

"Summarize my day" no longer makes things up

The home screen suggestion "Summarize what we did today" went through the light mode, with no history and no tools: Jimy answered with unrelated old memories. These requests now take the full path, and Jimy lists your recent conversations with their date before answering. Same fix for "Analyze this folder".

Also since 0.2.2

Version 0.2.3, released on October 22, delivered what the previous article promised:

  • The conversation list next to the Chat, with unread messages, and a side menu that folds away when the window is small.
  • The voice of your choice: Fish Audio (through OpenRouter), Windows voices (free and offline, no key), an OpenAI-compatible service such as a local Kokoro, or ElevenLabs.
  • "One brain for everything": a switch to use the same AI model everywhere, and a card showing what Jimy thinks with.
  • The complete English translation, down to Jimy's spoken answers and status sounds.
  • Tasks that no longer get lost: a background task interrupted by a restart is picked up again, and the home screen offers to resume your open work.
  • Real projects: Jimy can attach a conversation to a project and then reads its instructions (AGENTS.md). Say "new conversation" out loud to change the subject.
  • More security: another service's key showing up in a tool result is masked before it reaches the model.

How to update

  • You already have Jimy: run the new installer over the old one. Conversations, settings and memories are kept; your soul is created on first launch, identical to the character Jimy had so far.
  • First install: download the installer below. Jimy is free and open source, and your data stays on your PC.
