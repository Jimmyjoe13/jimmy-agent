Jimy 0.2.2: a new interface, English support and conversations that finally stay tidy

Published October 22, 2026 · By Jimmyjoe13 · 5 min read · Lire en français

Jimy is an open source desktop AI agent for Windows that you drive by voice or by text. For 0.2.2, we started by looking at how it is really used: weeks of logs, hundreds of conversations. The verdict was clear: Jimy did his job well, but finding your way around was hard. This release tidies the house.

In short

  • A new “Visor” interface in the logo's colors, with Jimy's live state at the top of every screen.
  • Jimy speaks English: pick the language in Settings; the first screens are translated, the rest is coming.
  • Tidier conversations: automatic titles, search across your whole history, pin, rename, archive.
  • More responsive: simple questions take a light path, and the voice starts sooner.
  • A more reliable memory that knows which memories actually help.

An interface in Jimy's colors: the “Visor”

The old interface was clean, but it looked like a developer tool. The new one takes the logo's colors, with one simple rule you will find everywhere:

The gradient is Jimy Coral is you Night blue keeps it calm
  • The “Visor” bar at the top of every screen shows what Jimy is doing right now: idle, listening, thinking, speaking. A light line moves while he works.
  • Two switches always at hand: “Jimy speaks” (mute his voice, he then answers in text only) and “Mic” (turn listening off).
  • The active brain, the AI model in use, is always visible, and one click takes you to its setting.
  • Clear tab names: Chat, Voice, Memory, Tools, Appearance, Archives, Settings.

Jimy speaks English

This was the most important step to open Jimy to everyone. In Settings › Language, choose Français, English, or Automatic, which follows your Windows language.

To be honest: in 0.2.2, navigation, the Visor bar, Jimy's states and the Archives are translated. The other screens, then Jimy's spoken answers, come in the next releases. Translation is checked automatically: a release cannot ship if a single English text is missing.

Conversations that finally stay tidy

In real use, almost one conversation out of two was simply called “Voice session” or “New session”. Impossible to find anything. 0.2.2 fixes that:

  • Automatic titles: every conversation gets a real title, then Jimy suggests a shorter one once the topic is clear. Old conversations were renamed during the update.
  • Search across your whole history: titles, projects, summaries and message content, no longer just the last 50 conversations.
  • Pin, rename, archive from the Archives. A title you choose never changes by itself.
  • Every answer stays in its conversation: if you switch conversations while Jimy is answering, his reply no longer shows up in the wrong place, and you are told it is ready.
  • Projects keep their own tasks: when you work on one project, Jimy no longer brings up another project's open tasks, and tasks idle for two weeks go to sleep.

More responsive, especially by voice

  • A light path for simple questions: a question that needs no tool (no file, no web, no action) goes out with a much shorter context. The answer comes faster and costs less.
  • The voice starts sooner: Jimy prepares the audio of his first sentence as soon as it is written, while he writes the rest, instead of waiting for the full answer.

A more reliable memory

Jimy now knows which memories he actually uses to answer you: the groundwork for keeping what helps and letting the rest go. We also removed stray memories left behind by our automated tests.

How to update

  • You already have Jimy: download the new installer and run it over the old one. Your conversations, settings and memories are kept (they live in %APPDATA%\Jimmy).
  • First install: download the installer below. Jimy is free and open source, and your data stays on your PC.

What's next?

0.2.2 lays the foundations. Here is what is coming:

  • a conversation list next to the Chat, showing what is running, what awaits your approval and unread messages;
  • picking your AI model in three clicks (provider, key, model), with clear labels: free, fast, offline;
  • the voice you want: Windows voices (free and offline), any OpenAI-compatible service or ElevenLabs, on top of today's voices;
  • a home screen to pick up where you left off, and real projects;
  • full English translation, all the way to Jimy's voice.
