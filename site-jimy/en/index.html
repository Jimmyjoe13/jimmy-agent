How It Works

Not just another chatbot: your autonomous desktop AI agent.

Jimy lives on your desktop, floating above your active windows. Move him around, he dodges your cursor, and visually reacts to everything he automates.

  1. 01

    You call him

    “Jimy…” — he answers “Yes?”. The wake word is detected locally on your machine: zero mic audio leaves your computer.

  2. 02

    He thinks

    Using the language model of your choice with your own API key: OpenCode Go, OpenRouter, Claude, DeepSeek, or local Ollama.

  3. 03

    He acts

    Files, shell commands, browser control, and MCP servers. Any sensitive action — sending, paying, deleting — requires your green light.

  4. 04

    He answers

    Out loud, with an animated speech bubble. And he retains what matters for your next session.

Get Started

Install Jimy on Windows: three minutes and you're set.

  1. Run the installer

    Jimy-setup.exe. If Windows alerts “Windows protected your PC”: click More info, then Run anyway.

  2. Equip his voice

    On first launch, it downloads the local speech recognition model (~65 MB). Voice listening activates immediately after.

  3. Connect your model

    Add your API key (OpenCode Go or OpenRouter). It stays stored locally on your machine.

Windows Defender blocking the installer? It's temporary: SignPath certification in progress.

Jimy is 100% free and open source (verifiable code on GitHub). Our official code signing certification is currently being processed by SignPath Foundation (see our code signing policy). Until our cryptographic key is assigned, Windows SmartScreen displays a blue cautionary screen because the executable is new.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Jimy.

Is it really free?

The desktop app and five default skins are 100% free under the MIT open source license. You only pay for your own language model token usage with your provider (OpenCode Go, OpenRouter, Claude, or free local models via Ollama).

Does it listen to me continuously?

The microphone listens locally for the wake word “Jimy”. All audio processing happens on your machine via Whisper: raw audio never leaves your computer.

What hardware is needed?

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Jimy is tested and runs smoothly on a standard laptop with 16 GB of RAM, without requiring a dedicated graphics card.

Why does Windows show a security warning?

The installer is awaiting certification from SignPath Foundation (Code signing policy). In the meantime, executables built via GitHub Actions trigger SmartScreen simply due to novelty. Follow our unblock guide: see here.

What about macOS?

macOS support is planned on our roadmap. For now, Jimy is optimized exclusively for Windows.

How do I activate a premium skin?

Once the store opens, you will receive a license key after purchase. In the app, navigate to the Skin tab → “Premium Skins”, paste your key and click Activate. The skin downloads automatically.
